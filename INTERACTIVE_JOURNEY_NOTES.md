# Interactive Gig Journey

The artistic live-music trail now supports:

- click-and-drag panning with a grab/grabbing cursor;
- mouse-wheel zoom centred on the pointer;
- touch dragging on phones and tablets;
- a Centre trail control to restore the original view;
- protection against accidentally opening a stop after dragging;
- automatic reset when the date range or city filter changes.

The real Leaflet venue map below remains independently draggable and zoomable.
